Scheduling priorities

Hard rules: every required slot should be covered; PRU has two required residents every day; approved leave blocks assignment; one assignment per person per day; eligibility, the full post-call calendar day is protected after each on-call, shift-credit caps, monthly on-call caps, consecutive-day limits, locked assignments, ≥3-calendar-day on-call spacing, daytime unit-continuity blocks, and all-staff weekend limits are protected.

Shift-credit logic: a daytime assignment counts as 1 shift credit; an on-call/night assignment counts as 2 shift credits. Each person's effective monthly credit maximum is the lower of the global rule and that person's individual maximum in Staff.

Weekend logic for all staff: when the selected month contains 4 weekends, every active staff member may cover at most 2 weekends; when it contains 5 weekends, the maximum is 3 weekends. A weekend package is either one weekend on-call or both weekend day shifts (Friday + Saturday in Saudi weekend mode). If automatic weekend capacity is short, only the minimum unavoidable weekend on-call slots are left open for manual completion.

Soft goals: after mandatory coverage is safe, assign extra capacity to ER first and then CCRT; honor requested free days and shift preferences; balance shift credits, on-calls, Fridays/Saturdays, and distinct weekends; and keep re-optimization changes to a minimum.
